{%- comment -%} The notice shown on any page that displays model-to-glossary mappings. It leads with the milestone - a first public release, agreed by the editors and open for review - rather than with a classification or with a list of what has not happened. The maturity level itself is metadata, carried on the resource as the FHIR structuredefinition-fmm extension (currently 1), which is where a classification belongs. Deliberately avoided: describing how the mappings were produced, which invites questions this release cannot answer; saying a review has not happened, which advertises a gap rather than a stage; and telling implementers not to rely on it, which undercuts the reason for publishing. "Informative" carries the whole weight of what the mapping does not bind. The earlier "no conformance requirement" said the same thing in the register of a liability notice. Not "draft" and not "experimental": in FHIR those say the resource is unfinished and authored for testing rather than genuine use, neither of which is true. The resource carries status active and experimental false. One include, so the wording cannot drift between pages and lifting it is a single edit. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = include.lang | default: page.lang | default: 'en' -%}
