{%- comment -%} Shown on every page when site.restricted is true. What this is, and what it is not. It keeps the site out of search results and tells a reader the content is not published yet. It does NOT restrict access: the pages, and the JSON under _resources/, remain reachable by anyone with the URL. A password prompt written in the site's own JavaScript would not change that - the content is served as plain files - so none is offered here, and nothing on the page claims protection the site cannot provide. Real access control is a web-server setting, not a site feature. See the "Restricting access" section of DELIVERY-NOTE.md. Turn off by setting `restricted: false` in _config.yml, which also drops the noindex meta and restores robots.txt. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = page.lang | default: 'en' -%}
